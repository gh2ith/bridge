# Bridgeway workflow rules

## UX design criteria

Apply the user's UX laws and principles to design decisions: Aesthetic-Usability Effect, Hick's Law, Fitts's Law, Jakob's Law, Miller's Law, Doherty Threshold, Law of Proximity, Law of Similarity, Law of Common Region, Visual Hierarchy, Progressive Disclosure, Recognition over Recall, Von Restorff Effect, Peak-End Rule, Goal-Gradient Effect, Serial Position Effect, Tesler's Law, Postel's Law, Occam's Razor, and Feedback Loops.

Use familiar interaction patterns, clear reading order, grouped information, restrained choices, accessible targets, visible progress, prompt feedback, and recovery paths that preserve entered work. Keep the primary action distinct. Apply these principles together in context; avoid adding controls, decoration, or steps solely to demonstrate a principle.

## Workflow authority

- Sidebar navigation uses small dots for unseen activity, never numeric totals or directory counts. Opening the relevant section clears its indicator for that viewer; later activity may show it again. Keep page-level statistics separate from these indicators.

- All accounts are created and maintained by administrators; there is no public self-registration. Platform Admin manages company and college administrator accounts. College admins manage their own student and supervisor accounts, including complete identity, contact, academic/organization, and access details. Local account records are not backend authentication; never persist passwords in frontend storage.
- Creation forms collect only the requested essentials. Staff IDs are generated automatically, office/room and office-hours fields are omitted during creation, and new accounts default to Active without a status selector. Keep the other role-specific fields. Status and optional office details belong in subsequent editing, not account creation. All creation entry points use the shared account editor.

These rules reflect the user's explicit clarification. Read them before changing workflow text, statuses, or behavior.

- The host company reviews internship applications and accepts or rejects applicants to its own listings.
- Applying creates a pending application, not automatic acceptance or placement.
- Companies may add application questions to opportunities; students must answer them when applying. Preserve submitted questions and answers with each application. Students may apply to multiple internships; company acceptance closes their other pending applications automatically.
- Company and student task PDFs are downloadable browser-local attachments, subject to file validation and storage limits; never describe these as server uploads.
- Companies may request interviews for their own pending applications. Students confirm or request another time. Interviews belong to Applications, never the training logbook, and do not change the hiring decision or create a placement. No invitation is actually transmitted by the local frontend.
- Academic supervisors do not approve, reject, or endorse internship applications. Do not add a faculty/college approval stage to the hiring decision.
- The college can assign or change an academic supervisor for any registered student, even before an application or company acceptance. This academic assignment does not accept applications or activate training. Placement administration and training activation still happen only after company acceptance; this is not a second hiring decision.
- Platform Admin can pause or resume any partnership. College admins can pause or resume only partnerships linked to their own college. Companies cannot change partnership status. Resuming requires both organization accounts to be active; existing training and history remain intact when paused.
- College admins view company listing details read-only and may pause/resume a listing only for their own college’s students. They cannot edit/delete or globally pause company listings. Existing applications and training remain intact. For temporary cycle testing, local availability restrictions persist in localStorage and affect discovery only for that college.
- The company records weekly training reports and submits the end-of-term evaluation.
- Internship publication now requires Platform Admin approval. Approved listings are offered to all active colleges, subject to a college's local availability restriction; company acceptance of applicants remains a separate decision.
- Companies create and edit eight-week training plans with checklist objectives and evidence. Supervisors review objective results and use one supervisor-wide report-visibility control for all assigned students and all weeks, including future weeks. Do not add per-student or per-week visibility controls. Restriction hides company feedback, objective results, review notes, and individual weekly report statuses (including badges and week selectors) without hiding the plan or changing verification or verified totals.
- Platform Admin oversees student placement histories, listing moderation, reports, and threaded support tickets, with filtered export of sample records. Student tickets go to the assigned academic supervisor; supervisor tickets go to their college administrator; company and college-administrator tickets go to Platform Admin. Requesters retain their own conversations; only the receiving role manages ticket status. Student tickets without an assigned supervisor wait for assignment. Ticket inboxes, counts, and exports must respect this routing. Tickets start In progress; a receiving handler’s reply automatically sets Awaiting requester, and the requester’s reply returns the ticket to In progress. There is no manual status dropdown; the receiving handler can explicitly resolve a ticket only after sending a reply, followed by a separate confirmation with no additional text required. Resolved tickets are closed and read-only for everyone; replies must never reopen them.
- For temporary cycle testing, the connected workspace uses localStorage on this browser and origin. Existing Bridgeway session records migrate without overwriting newer local records. Workflow actions, account records, profiles, assignments, restrictions, preferences, CV metadata, and task PDFs survive reloads; open tabs reload safe views or offer to load newer changes when drafts are open. This is not backend persistence, authentication, external notifications, or organization-wide synchronization. Never persist passwords.
- The company is the author of student ratings and weekly feedback. The assigned supervisor reviews and approves company-submitted reports, verifies their reported hours, or requests revisions; supervisors never create an independent student rating or score objectives. Approval must not require supervisor-entered ratings. These actions must never be described as application approval.
- Company evaluations remain read-only to supervisors. After the company submits its final evaluation, the assigned academic supervisor adds a separate academic assessment and forwards it to the college administrator. The college reviews both evaluations and a completion checklist before approving completion and issuing the existing blue-and-silver Bridgeway certificate. This separate final academic assessment does not let supervisors score company objectives or change company ratings or weekly reports.
- Task submissions retain version history, submitted descriptions/links/PDF references, company feedback, and revision requests. The company can end and submit a week only after every active task in that week has a latest company review of Completed; supervisor verification enforces the same check. Submitted and verified weeks are read-only for task creation, editing, removal, attachments, student submissions, and company task reviews; draft weeks and supervisor-requested revisions allow task work. Task completion never automatically verifies training hours. Completion requires all active tasks completed, all eight reports verified, 120 verified hours (8 weeks × 15 hours; company ends and submits each week, supervisor approval adds its 15 hours to the verified total), company evaluation, academic assessment meeting requirements, and college administrator confirmation.
- Student navigation is a sidebar; retain the student's name and account dropdown for profile and sign out. Student-selected profile skills are copied into applications at submission time, without fabricated AI matching scores.
- The user has removed preview, beta, demo, and sample branding from the production-facing interface. Keep labels neutral and task-focused, remove demo-only controls, and do not reintroduce simulation banners. This copy policy does not mean backend integration is complete: never claim a server save, upload, credential change, notification, or organization-wide synchronization that did not happen.
- Planned AI matching compares the student's CV with each internship's requirements. A CV match is role fit, not acceptance probability. Do not add a "Why this match?" feature. Until AI is connected, do not display fabricated match percentages at all. CV selection currently retains only filename and size in localStorage, never document contents or real analysis.
- Historical reports, screenshots, and backup archives can contain superseded workflows. They are not a source of authority over these rules.
